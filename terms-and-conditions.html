Genuine Leather Standards & Natural Characteristics

Zakara International proudly crafts 100% genuine real leather goods, using genuine cowhide, buffalo hide, calfskin, pull-up, Hunter vintage, and vegetable-tanned leathers. Because genuine leather is a biological, organic material, every hide possesses inherent natural traits that differentiate it from synthetic, polyurethane (PU), or faux substitutes.

Intrinsic Leather Attributes (Non-Defect Policy)

  • Natural Surface Markings: Subtle grain variations, healed scratches, natural insect bites, neck wrinkles, and pore variations are authentic markers of full-grain and top-grain hides, not defects.
  • Tannage & Color Shifts: Small tonal differences between different dye lots or hide sections can occur due to natural hide porosity and manual drum-dyeing processes.
  • Pull-Up & Patina Formation: Premium oil-tanned and wax-treated pull-up leathers temporarily lighten when folded or stressed, developing a coveted vintage patina with age.
  • Artisanal Dimensional Tolerances: Hand-cut and artisan-stitched products carry an acceptable dimensional variance tolerance of approximately ±3% to ±5% across finished silhouettes.
Natural Leather Disclosure: By purchasing or ordering leather products from Zakara International, the Client acknowledges that variations in grain texture, tone, and surface markings are intrinsic proofs of genuine authenticity and will not constitute valid grounds for warranty defect claims or chargebacks.

We specialize in custom design, private label branding, and OEM/ODM manufacturing for boutique designers, luxury labels, corporate enterprises, and global retailers. All custom manufacturing adheres to the following structured lifecycle:

Export Logistics, Incoterms & International Customs Duties

Zakara International exports handcrafted leather goods to clients across North America (USA, Canada), the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia, Scandinavia, Japan, and the Middle East. Shipments are governed by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) Incoterms® 2020 guidelines stipulated on your Proforma Invoice:

  • EXW (Ex-Works, Kanpur Factory): The Buyer assumes full shipping responsibility and cost upon cargo readiness at our Kanpur factory dock.
  • FOB (Free On Board, Nhava Sheva / Mundra / New Delhi): Zakara International bears all Indian export clearance charges and freight delivery to the nominated port/airport. Maritime or air cargo risks transfer to Buyer once loaded aboard the vessel.
  • CIF / CFR (Cost, Insurance & Freight): Zakara International books and prepays sea freight/air freight to the nominated port of destination.
  • Express Courier (Air Courier via DHL, FedEx, UPS): Door-to-door transit for prototypes, corporate samples, and priority small consignments.

Unless explicitly invoiced under Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) terms, all destination import tariffs, customs duties, value-added taxes (VAT/GST), excise fees, quarantine inspections, and local handling surcharges are the sole legal responsibility of the Buyer. Zakara International is not responsible for transit delays caused by customs clearance, import license verifications, or port strikes in the destination country.

Every production lot undergoes rigorous multi-tier quality control prior to export packing, examining structural stitching density, rivet tensile strength, zipper glides, edge-coating adhesion, and lining reinforcement.

Mandatory Claim Notification Windows

  • Wholesale / B2B Consignments: The Buyer must conduct incoming quality inspection and lodge any formal claims regarding manufacturing defects or discrepancies within 14 calendar days of customs release at the destination port.
  • Retail / Web Orders: Any discrepancies or damage sustained in transit must be reported within 7 calendar days of courier delivery.
  • Evidence Submission Requirement: All claims must be accompanied by the original Commercial Invoice number, carton tracking numbers, high-resolution photographs, and short video clips demonstrating the alleged manufacturing flaw.

Craftsmanship Warranty & Professional Leather Care

Zakara International stands behind the artisanal integrity of its goods with a 1-Year Limited Craftsmanship Warranty covering structural defects arising during normal, intended personal and professional usage.

Warranty Inclusions

Defects covered include seam separation, structural stitching unraveling, rivet detachment, broken metal D-rings or snap hooks, and zipper track failure attributable to manufacturing error.

Warranty Exclusions

  • Normal cosmetic wear and tear, surface abrasion, corner scuffing, or patina darkening caused by natural oils and usage.
  • Water submersion damage, mildew from moisture-rich storage, chemical solvent stains, or unauthorized alterations.
  • Physical misuse, exceeding structural load limits, or cuts induced by sharp objects.
Recommended Care: Keep authentic leather away from intense direct sunlight, prolonged humidity, and abrasive solvents. Use a reputable neutral leather balm or conditioner every 6 to 12 months to maintain suppleness.
