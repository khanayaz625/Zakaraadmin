Zakara International is a custom leather bag manufacturer in India, working towards a common mission of developing custom designed leather bags that help our clients build their brands with confidence. Headquartered in Kanpur, we have built a global presence through handmade leather craftsmanship for markets across North and South America, Switzerland, the Netherlands, Poland, the Czech Republic, Australia, Finland, Norway, and Sweden.

With over 12 years of experienced craftsmanship in customised leather goods manufacturing, we have stood the test of time because of our patterns, designs, stitching, and our 100% real leather products which are today being praised globally as well. As a result, our clients receive consistent quality across every order.

  • Production Capacity: We can make 2500-3000 handbags and travel bags, and 4000-5000 wallets and small items every month.
  • Types of Leather: We use buffalo, cow, calf, and sheep leather.
  • Leather Finishes: Our leather can be drum dyed, vegetable tanned, aniline, soft nappa, wax, and oil tanned.
  • Skilled Craftsmanship: Our products are made by skilled artisans and craftsmen, ensuring top quality.
  • Target Market: As a leather bag manufacturer in India, we focus on the USA, Canada, Europe, and Scandinavian countries.
